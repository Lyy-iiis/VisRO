**Correctness check.** Decodes both panels' heightmaps from the PNG pixels (own iso projection and painter; meta supplies only camera, palette and layout), requires them to equal the meta heightmaps, and recomputes added = sum(max(0, after-before)) and removed = sum(max(0, before-after)) in the form the question asks (added / added+removed / 'added,removed'); the question must equal the declared template, contain no digit and carry only the placeholder format 'added,removed'; each decode window must equal the render of its decoded heightmap pixel for pixel, and every pixel outside the windows must equal the documented chrome (BEFORE label left, AFTER label right, frames, separator, white).

**Uniqueness check.** An exhaustive front-to-back decoder over every height of every floor cell (pad row/column included, up to 'above the panel') must find exactly ONE heightmap per panel that reproduces the panel pixels exactly, so no other count is consistent with the image. The camera is pinned by the image: the whole floor grid, including its always-empty, unoccluded front pad strip, must lie inside the compared window. In addition every column top shows >= 8 robust px, and every single-column change and every change of two overlapping columns (nearer one lowered) alters a visible patch (contrast 20, >= 24 px).
